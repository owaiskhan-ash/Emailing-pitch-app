## How to read the contact columns

- **Outlet/venue-specific** means the published address is tied to a particular outlet or venue, including an outlet manager or restaurant dining address.
- **Shared brand/hotel** means the address serves a wider brand, hotel, reservations, banquet, sales, support, feedback, or other central function. It is not necessarily a direct restaurant mailbox.
- The detailed **contact scope** column preserves that distinction; for example, `Outlet`, `Outlet manager`, `Reservations`, `Banquets`, or `Shared brand customer care`.
- A public listing and valid syntax do **not** prove that a mailbox is still monitored or can receive mail. Confirm important leads through the linked source, venue phone number, or contact form before outreach.
